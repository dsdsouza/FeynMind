# FeynMind 0.2.0: Retention

**Early preview.** FeynMind now schedules reviews so you keep what you learn. Your data from 0.1.0 is upgraded the first time you open 0.2.0, and nothing is lost: your materials, concepts, lessons, answers, grades and mastery all carry over. If you want a way back, copy your data folder before you upgrade (`%APPDATA%\FeynMind` on Windows, `~/Library/Application Support/FeynMind` on macOS, `~/.config/FeynMind` on Linux).

## New

- **Spaced reviews.** Every concept you master gets its own review schedule, timed to when you are about to forget it (the FSRS algorithm). Concepts you mastered in 0.1.0 are scheduled from the day you mastered them.
- **Reviews first.** The Learn screen shows what is due today and why. A sitting opens with the due reviews, mixed across topics, before any new lesson. A daily limit (25 by default, changeable) keeps a backlog after missed days manageable, most at-risk first; you can skip the rest of today's reviews.
- **Recall, not re-reading.** A review asks a question without the explanation and without naming the concept. Questions vary: FeynMind asks one you have not seen recently, and writes new ones from the same book passages only when needed. "Show a hint" is there when you need it (it counts as help).
- **A missed review comes back sooner.** It returns the next day, and FeynMind offers a short re-teach of what you missed and one question to check it. Lessons that build on a missed concept wait until it is re-taught; lessons you already started carry on.
- **Earlier topics in lessons.** A lesson occasionally includes one question from an earlier concept.
- **Exam priorities.** When an exam from your syllabus is within two weeks, reviews and the next lesson favour its heaviest-weighted and weakest concepts, with the reason shown ("Quiz 5 in 6 days · equal weight · predicted recall on the day 64%"). Change exam dates and weights on the Learn screen.
- **Progress.** A new Progress tab shows how much you recall 7 and 30 days after learning (the first review 4 to 13 and 21 to 45 days after; FeynMind moves a review into those windows when it would otherwise skip them), reviews per day, what is coming up, and each concept's memory.
- **Works offline.** What is due, your history and your progress work without a connection. A review answered offline is saved and graded when you are back online.
- **Cost.** A review is one model call (its grade); new review questions and a re-teach cost one or two more, shown before they are sent. The estimate for today's reviews is shown before you start.

## Fixed

- **Saving an edited syllabus erased processed units (data loss).** In 0.1.0, editing and saving a confirmed syllabus unlinked each processed unit's concepts from it and deleted its processing record and any "I know this unit" check, so lessons and progress disappeared from the Learn screen. Saving now updates units in place and keeps everything. If this happened to you, re-importing does not recover it; please report it.
- **The Learn screen failed when no model was chosen.** With processed units but no model selected in Settings, the Learn screen showed an error. It now shows your path with a message and an **Open Settings** button; lessons, the diagnostic and reviews ask for a model before they start.

## Known limitations

- **Gemini only.** Google Gemini is the only provider, and one model does every job. More providers and a model per role are next.
- **Reviews use standard settings.** The schedule uses FSRS's default parameters for everyone; it does not yet tune itself to your own memory.
- **Known is not yet learned.** Concepts you mark as known in the diagnostic or with "I know this unit" get a review schedule only once a lesson masters them.
- **Re-teach.** It is offered in the sitting where you missed the review; if you skip it, a miss the next day offers it again.
- **Importing a different syllabus file** still replaces the old syllabus and its units (editing and saving the same syllabus is safe).
- **Time zone changes** are picked up when the window regains focus; this has not been tested on every system.
- **English only.** Search, text recognition of scanned pages, and dictation are English-only.
- **One course at a time.** Pathways, multiple courses and the learning map are planned.
- **Apple silicon Macs only**, and **unsigned installers**: Windows and macOS warn before the first launch (see the README).
- **macOS and Linux install steps are untested**, and key storage on Linux without a keyring is unverified.
- **Preview quality.** Grading has been checked on small benchmarks (32 cases for lessons, 6 for review questions), not at scale; the vision model can misread small details; spoken math has not been tested; only .pptx (not .ppt) decks are read.
